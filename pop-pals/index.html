How to play

  1. Pick a place and a speed. You look down at the holes.
  2. Animals pop up. Tap one that lives in that place. It goes back down and makes its sound. Its name shows on it.
  3. On Tiny Tots and Little Pals, green means tap and red means leave it.
  4. Other animals, and people, add an oops. Fill the oops dots and the burrow goes boing. Everyone flies out.
  5. Send home the number of pals on the counter before the timer ends.
  6. On Ranger Rush, a safari bus, submarine, bubble, pet van, or forest jeep rolls past and hides the holes. Wait, then tap.
