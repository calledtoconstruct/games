# Gaze and input: five games

Date: 2026-10-18. Gaze-and-input ruling for Sudoku, math practice, word search, flash cards, and the maze. Mouse, touch, and gaze do the same acts. This memo does not restate puzzle generation. Where a research preference fights a required loop, the loop wins, and the pick is marked.

Bands, used everywhere: Tiny Tots about 2–3, Little Pals about 4–5, Shopkeeper about 6–8, Rush about 9 and up.

## Shared clock

One pointer. It is the mouse, the touch, or the smoothed gaze. Gaze smoothing matches the shipped horse game: each sample moves 0.45 of the way toward the new point. There is no blink and no dwell setting.

Socket `ws://127.0.0.1:7081`. On every open, send the nine hello bytes `1, 4, 0, 0, 0, 0, 5, 0, 0`. An https page cannot open that socket. Ship the page on http. If the socket is down, hide the gaze mark, retry the socket every 2 seconds, and let mouse and touch play the whole game with no wait and no missing control.

Play dwell is 650 ms from the moment the pointer enters a live play target. Menu dwell is 1300 ms from entry. Do not add time on top of those totals, and do not lengthen them for children. Size does the work.

| Moment | When | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Onset | 0 ms through 199 ms | Ring stays at 0%. No speech. No commit. No maze trail. No word-search preview. |
| Ring | 200 ms until commit | One 54 px ring follows the pointer. Fill is 0% at 200 ms and 100% at 650 ms (play) or 1300 ms (menu): fraction `(t − 200) / (D − 200)`. The target does not grow. |
| Latch | t = 650 or 1300, once per visit | The act below fires once. The ring holds at 100% until the pointer leaves. Staying does not fire again. |
| Release | Pointer leaves the target before commit | Ring returns to 0%. That visit is discarded. A latch already completed (Sudoku cell, word-search anchor) stays. |
| Cancel | Same as release, plus the overrides below | In-progress dwell dies. Queued speech for that visit never plays, because speech starts only at latch. |

Cancel also happens when a pointer button goes down, when the latest gaze sample is 500 ms old or older, or when the socket closes. Those cancel the ring only. They do not clear a Sudoku latch or a word-search anchor, and they do not reset a round.

Pointer press overrides gaze for the whole press, including a press on empty space. Extra pointers are ignored until that press ends. A click or a tap on a live target is the same act as a finished dwell, with a wait of 0 ms. It is one step, not a drag. Pressing on one target and releasing on another commits only the target that receives the click. While the button is down, gaze must not fire.

The ring is the horse game’s mark: 54 px, centered on the pointer, green fill `#2f8a3e` over paper `rgba(244, 240, 216, 0.45)`, events ignored so it is not a target. It is visible whenever gaze is fresh. Off a live target the fill stays 0%. Departure from that game, on purpose: there the ring starts on the first frame. Here the first 200 ms draw an empty ring so a glance does not look like a choice. Do not copy that game’s 280 ms “faster” release. Leaving a target cancels at once.

Play versus menu is an attribute on the control, same test as the horse game. A play control dwells 650 ms. Every other control dwells 1300 ms. Mute, fullscreen, and Back to menu are always menu controls, including during a round. The Eyes chip is not a control.

Family rule for a miss: a look that is not a finished dwell on a live target changes no game state. A finished dwell that is not a legal success does not reset the round, does not clear committed progress, does not buzz, does not say “wrong” or “no,” and does not change a score. There is no score.

Size law:

- Hard floor: every control’s short side is at least 72 px, including at 390 px wide. Under 320 px wide, do not shrink and do not pretend the layout fits.
- Comfort floor: 112 px. Cards, maze stones, box choosers, and tray symbols use this unless a listed 390 exception says otherwise.
- Checked narrow window: 390 × 700. Narrow layout starts under 480 px wide. Page pad is 8 px. Gap between tiled cells is 8 px, or 4 px only when 8 px would push a cell under 72 px. Gap between choice cards is at least 16 px, and 24 px when it fits.
- Hit boxes match the painted controls. Do not inflate grid cells or cards. Maze stones may add at most 8 px of padding, and two lit hit boxes still need at least 8 px of dead floor between them.
- Stones, cards, tray symbols, box choosers, and paging buttons sit at least 16 px in from the screen edge. Menu chrome may sit 8 px in. A tiled letter or Sudoku cell may use the page pad below, because a 16 px inset cannot hold five 72 px columns in 390 px.
- Nothing important is a drag or a thin path. Gaze never traces a corridor, a stroke, or a diagonal.

## Conflicts, and the one rule

**Onset added versus onset inside the dwell.** Sudoku and the maze quote a 150–250 ms silent onset and then a further 650–850 ms of feedback. Math, flash cards, and word search time the whole act at about 650 ms, and the product totals are fixed. Rule: onset is 200 ms inside the 650 or the 1300, measured from entry. Commit does not move.

**Ring on the cursor versus a contracting mark on the target.** The maze and Sudoku notes want a mark that contracts on the target. A gaze study cited for flash cards found that highlighting the target slowed selection, and that magnifying it did not help. The shipped game already draws one ring on the pointer. Rule: that one 54 px ring only. Do not magnify. A maze trail and a word-search preview are allowed after onset. They are not a second ring and they do not commit.

**Child dwells of 0.8 s, 1 s, or 1.5 s versus 650 ms.** The longer numbers are real for small targets and new gaze users. Rule: keep 650 and 1300. If looks miss, the target is too small, too close, or too near the edge. Do not add a penalty and do not stretch the timer.

**72 px versus about 3 cm versus about 6 cm.** The shipped floor is 72 px, about 19 mm, and the Sudoku note says that is small for a gaze digit. Word search wants cells near 6 cm and treats under about 3 cm as a failed child size. A 6 cm cell is about 227 px and does not fit a multi-column grid in 390 px. Rule: 72 px is the hard floor, 112 px (about 3 cm) is the comfort floor, and 6 cm is not required. Dense grids may sit between 72 and 112 only in the exceptions below. Never use 64 px.

**What a finished miss does.** Math leaves the blank and waits. Flash-card research shows the right pair and moves on. Word search moves the anchor and does not score. The maze ignores the look. Sudoku never offers an illegal write. Rule: a miss never advances the round, never resets it, and never scolds. Sudoku: no-op. Math: same blank, that card inert until the pointer leaves. Word search: the new cell becomes the anchor, found words stay. Flash cards: stay on the same trial; speak only whole words, never a rebuke, then outline the right card for 800 ms. Maze: the token stays. Local detail is under each game. The shared sentence is the rule.

**Speech on enter versus speech on commit.** Preview speech reads every card a look crosses, including the answer. Rule: no speech before latch. Leave during onset or ring and nothing is said. One utterance at a time. A new latch replaces the old utterance. Menus that leave or pick a mode stay silent. Dwelling a word reads that word at latch, which is the required flash-card loop and the word-search bank.

**Word-search bank at 1300 ms versus a flash-card word at 650 ms.** Both speak a word. They are not the same act. The bank must not score a find, and a sweep of many chips must not read the list. A flash-card word is the answer. Rule: a dwell that commits a play act is 650 ms, even if it also speaks. A dwell that only speaks a word in a bank of many words is 1300 ms. A single printed prompt that speaks and does not select is 650 ms, because it is one word the child asked for, not a list.

**Mouse wait.** Word search is explicit that a click has zero wait. The product says the acts are the same, not that the waits are the same. Rule: click and tap wait 0 ms. Gaze waits 650 or 1300. The state change is the same step.

**Early flash-card direction.** Research wants a spoken word, then a picture, for the young bands, and picture-then-print only later. The required loop is the reverse shape: show a picture, choose the word card, and reverse only in mature play. Rule: the loop wins. Tiny Tots and Little Pals never reverse. Shopkeeper reverses about 1 in 3 trials. Rush reverses about 1 in 2. Choice counts stay 2, 3, 4, and 4. Do not go to 6.

**Toddler math without an equation.** Research wants sets of 1–3 and no printed `+`, `−`, or `=`. The required loop is a partial equation with the missing value filled. Rule: every band has one blank and the child fills it from cards. Tiny Tots uses picture quantities in the slots and no operator glyphs. Little Pals and older print the equation. No band gets a 0–9 pad.

**Sudoku pictures versus digits.** The required loop says place digits with dwell, under real Sudoku rules, on age-scaled grids. Research uses four pictures before numerals. Rule: real rules on every band (each symbol once in the row, the column, and the box; one solution). Tiny Tots and Little Pals place four picture symbols. Shopkeeper and Rush place digits. The dwell act is the same. A 9×9 of cells under 112 px is not shown.

**Tray always visible versus a phone that cannot hold it.** Research keeps the tray on the page so candidates are not nine specks inside a cell. A 390 × 700 screen cannot hold a 6×6 or 9×9 and a large tray together. Rule: the tray is never marks inside the cell. On a 4×4 it stays on screen. On a 6×6 or 9×9 that does not meet the size test below, the child dwells box, then cell, then symbol, and the symbol screen may replace the grid for that step.

**Word search by dragging a diagonal versus two cells.** No report found a gaze word search. The constraint forbids tracing a thin path. Rule: two dwells, first cell and last cell, either order. The engine takes the straight segment. Preview does not commit.

**Maze by steering versus waypoints.** Pencil charts and corridor tracing lose. Rule: one dwell on the next lit stone. The token walks the open floor. The floor, the walls, and a stone two forks away are not targets.

## Sudoku

Place a symbol with dwell. Real Sudoku rules. Grids scale by band: Tiny Tots 4×4 pictures, 12 of 16 given, and each hole a naked single so the tray shows one symbol. Little Pals 4×4 pictures, 8–10 given. Shopkeeper 6×6 digits, 24–28 given. Rush 6×6 digits, 18–22 given, and a 9×9 of at least 40 givens only when the cells qualify. Singles only. No pencil marks, no clock, no mistake tally.

**Play targets, 650 ms.** An empty cell. A box, when the board is in box mode. A live tray symbol, and only while a cell is latched. Undo. Hint. The control that returns from the symbol screen or the zoomed box to the boxes, labeled so it is not Back to menu.

**Not targets.** A given cell. A dim illegal symbol. The latched cell’s interior does not fill from a look alone.

**Menu targets, 1300 ms.** Band, Back, a new board, and Another. Another appears only after the board matches the solution. Mute and fullscreen.

**Dwell.** Onset 200 ms, ring until 650 ms, latch as follows. Release and cancel clear the ring and do not clear a cell latch.

- Latch on an empty cell: that cell stays selected after the eyes leave. It does not fill. A later empty cell replaces the latch. A second visit to the same cell does not clear it and does not write.
- Latch on a live symbol while a cell is latched: write it, clear the latch, return to the grid if the tray had replaced it. No cheer unless the board is complete. The cheer is not a score.
- Latch on a live symbol with nothing latched: no write and no scold.
- Tiny Tots: the tray’s only button is the symbol that fits. Older bands: illegal symbols are visible, dim, and dead. Dead means no ring.
- Undo removes the last symbol the child placed. It does not remove a given.
- Hint latches one cell that is a single now and pulses that tray symbol. It does not write. The child still dwells the symbol.

**Wrong look.** Does nothing. An illegal symbol cannot be written, so there is no wrong placement to punish. Looking at a given, a dead symbol, or the background cancels the ring. Do not reset the board.

**Two-step.** Required: cell, then symbol. Never one look that both picks the cell and writes. On a 6×6 or 9×9 that fails the size test, add a box step first: box, then cell, then symbol.

**Size at 390 × 700.**

- 4×4 stays one screen. Cells are 87 px (4 columns, 8 px gaps, 8 px pad: `4×87 + 3×8 + 16 = 388`). Tray is 2×2 at 175 × 112, inset 16 px, 8 px between the buttons (`(390 − 32 − 8) / 2 = 175`). Undo and Hint are 175 × 72. Stack with 88 px of chrome is about 660 px tall, inside 700.
- Show a full 6×6 only when every cell is at least 112 px and every tray symbol is at least 112 px on that same screen. That cell size needs about 728 px of width before a beside-the-grid tray. Otherwise six box targets, short side at least 112, in a 3×2.
- Show a full 9×9 only when every cell is at least 112 px, about 1088 px of width. Otherwise nine box targets, short side at least 112, in a 3×3. At 390 those boxes are 114 px (`(390 − 32 − 16) / 3 = 114`), inset 16 px. The six 6×6 boxes use that same 114 px width.
- Zoomed cells are at least 72 px and 112 px when the tray still fits at 88 px or more. If the tray would fall under 88, do not shrink it. Replace the grid with a symbol screen until a symbol latches or the child dwells the return control. Six symbols are 3×2 at 114 × 160. Nine symbols are 3×3 at 114 × 112. Both clear the 112 px comfort floor.

## Math

A partial equation, one blank, filled from cards. No keypad, no drag, no clock, no tally.

| Band | On screen | Cards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tiny Tots | Picture quantities, one blank, no printed `+`, `−`, or `=` | 2 |
| Little Pals | Printed `a + b = □`, then subtraction, then a missing part. Totals 0–5 | 3 |
| Shopkeeper | Digits, `+` and `−`, missing result, then a part, then the start | 4 |
| Rush | `+`, `−`, `×`, and `÷`, still three terms and one blank | 4 |

**Play targets, 650 ms.** The answer cards only.

**Not a target.** The equation, the pictures in the stem, and the blank. Looking at the problem selects nothing.

**Menu targets, 1300 ms.** Band, Back, and Done. Mute and fullscreen.

**Dwell.** Onset 200, ring until 650, latch writes or refuses. There is no persistent latch. Release and cancel leave the equation as it was.

- Right card: write that value into the blank and leave it. Hold the finished line 700 ms, then the next challenge. No praise track and no score.
- Wrong card: the blank stays empty. That card ignores the pointer until it leaves, so a held look cannot fail again. The other cards stay live.

**Wrong look.** Does not reset the challenge, clear a previous right answer, or scold. Do not speak the number they missed.

**Two-step.** Not used. One dwell on one card.

**Size.** Card short side at least 112 px. At 390 × 700 do not put four cards in one row. Two cards stack. Three cards stack. Four cards are a 2×2 at 171 × 140: inset 16 px and a 16 px gutter, `(390 − 32 − 16) / 2 = 171`. The equation sits above the cards and may use the full width inside that inset. If a short window would push a card under 112 px, drop one card (4, then 3, then 2). Do not shrink the card.

## Word search

Mark a word in a condensed overlapping grid. Each answer string appears once. The child does not drag.

Directions a segment may use: Tiny Tots and Little Pals, right only. Shopkeeper, right and down. Rush, right, down, down-right, and down-left. Backward is off unless a harder Rush flag is on, and it is still two endpoints, not a drag.

Grids: Tiny Tots 4×3, three 3-letter words. Little Pals 5×4, four or five words of 3–4 letters. Shopkeeper 6×6, six to eight words of 4–5 letters. Rush 6×6 up to 7×6, eight words of 4–7 letters. Put the shorter side across when a narrow window needs larger cells, but only if the longest rightward word still fits. The bank sits under the grid, not beside it.

**Play targets, 650 ms.** A letter cell. Previous and Next, when the grid is paged.

**Menu targets, 1300 ms.** A bank chip: speak that word and, for Tiny Tots and Little Pals, show its picture. Do not mark the word found. Also band, theme, Back, mute, and fullscreen.

**Dwell.** Onset 200, ring until 650 on a cell. A rest highlight before 650 is not a choice. Preview starts at 200 ms, not at entry: while an anchor is set, cells on the straight allowed line from the anchor to the current cell may light if they are a prefix of one unfound word. Looking off that line clears the preview and keeps the anchor. Preview never commits by itself.

- Latch with no anchor: that cell becomes the anchor and stays anchored after release.
- Latch on the anchor cell again, after a real leave and a new 650 ms: clear the anchor.
- Latch on a second cell: if the segment, either order, is exactly one unfound answer in an allowed direction, lock the whole word, mark its bank chip, and clear the anchor. If it is not, do not buzz. The new cell becomes the anchor.
- A click or a tap is the same step with 0 ms wait. It is not a stroke.

Release and cancel of an unfinished dwell do not clear the anchor. Paging does not clear the anchor, does not mark a word, and does not reset the grid. After 20 seconds with no latch, one unfound word may pulse its first cell once, for 650 ms. Do not pulse at the start of the round. The pulse does not set the anchor.

**Wrong look.** A short look, a look on the gutter, or a look on a cell that does not finish 650 ms changes nothing. A finished look on a segment that is not an unfound answer does not reset found words and does not scold. It only moves the anchor. Do not speak on a bad segment.

**Two-step.** Required: first cell, then last cell. Do not score by dwelling the bank. Do not require the eyes to visit the middle letters.

**Size.** Prefer cells near 112 px or larger. Hard floor 72 px. Grow cells to fill the width. Do not add columns to use spare pixels.

**Revised 2026-10-19.** The size rules in this note, including the 72 px floor and “the document scrolls vertically rather than shrinking,” are superseded when they push a target off the glass. A look cannot select a control that is outside the viewport, and these games must not require scrolling. Word search shows every column. Cells, bank chips, group chips, and menu buttons scale to the largest size that keeps the chrome and the whole scene inside the window. Cells stop growing at 168 px. Group chips stop at 160 px. The 6 cm preference is kept only as a wish for the largest cell that still fits. Grid sizes and dwell times are unchanged.

At 390 × 700:

- 3 columns, 8 px gaps, 8 px pad: cells 119 px. Use this for Tiny Tots (3-letter words across, 4 rows).
- 4 columns: cells 87 px (`4×87 + 3×8 + 16 = 388`). Use this when the longest across-word is 4 letters.
- 5 columns: cells 73 px, gaps 4 px, side pad 4 px (`5×73 + 4×4 + 8 = 389`). Use this for a 5-letter across-word. Do not go under 72.
- 6 or 7 columns do not fit. Do not shrink. Show 4 columns at 87 px and page with Previous and Next, each at least 72 × 72. A 7-letter word may start on one page and end on the next. The anchor survives the page change. Preview lights only visible cells.
- Bank chips are at least 72 px tall. At 390, show four chips of a row at about 87 × 72 and page the rest. Do not put eight chips in one row.

## Flash cards

Show a picture. Choose the word card among the cards below. Mature play sometimes reverses to a printed word, then picture cards. Dwelling a word reads it aloud at latch, not on entry. No preview speech. No magnify. No recorded clips. On-device speech only.

| Band | Choices | Direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tiny Tots | 2 word cards | Picture, then word. No reverse. |
| Little Pals | 3 word cards | Picture, then word. No reverse. |
| Shopkeeper | 4 word cards | Picture, then word. About 1 in 3 trials reversed. |
| Rush | 4 word cards | Picture, then word. About 1 in 2 trials reversed. |

**Play targets, 650 ms.** A choice card. On a reverse trial, the printed prompt is also a play target, and latching it only speaks. It does not select a picture.

**Not a target.** The picture prompt. A picture choice never speaks.

**Menu targets, 1300 ms.** Subject, Stop, Back, credits. No speech on those.

**Dwell.** Onset 200, ring until 650, latch once. No persistent latch. Release and cancel speak nothing.

- Word card: speak that word at latch. If it is the right choice, show picture and word together for 700 ms, then the next trial. The spoken word is the confirmation. No extra praise.
- Wrong word card: the trial stays. Speak the dwelled word, then the correct word once. Both utterances are the bare word. Outline the right card for 800 ms. That card can be dwelled during the outline. The wrong card stays inert until the pointer leaves. Do not advance on the miss.
- Picture choice on a reverse trial: no speech. A right picture holds the pair 700 ms, then the next trial. A wrong picture stays on the trial, outlines the right picture for 800 ms, and does not buzz. Speak the correct word once at that miss, and not before.
- Do not speak the answer when the trial appears.

**Wrong look.** Does not reset the deck, skip the trial, or scold.

**Two-step.** Not used. One dwell chooses. The prompt speak is a hint, not a first step.

**Size.** Same card law as math. Short side at least 112 px. At 390 × 700, two and three cards stack, four cards are a 2×2 at 171 × 140. The prompt picture is large, above the cards, inside the 16 px inset, and is not a choice. If four cards would fall under 112 px, drop to three. Do not go to six, and do not shrink.

## Maze

Traverse by dwelling the next stone. The eyes do not follow the corridor. One open route. Walking into a dead end is legal. The way back is the stone that lights behind the token. No timer that can fail. No reset to the start.

Lit stones at once: Tiny Tots 1, the next stone only, over a solution of 2–4 dwells. Little Pals 2, over 4–7 dwells. Shopkeeper up to 3, over 8–14 dwells. Rush up to 3, over 12–20 dwells. Unlit junctions are not targets. A straight run is one move, not a bead per cell.

**Play targets, 650 ms.** A lit stone, and nothing else in the maze.

**Not targets.** Walls, floor, the token, an unlit junction, a junction two forks away, and the trail.

**Menu targets, 1300 ms.** Band, Back, and Another. Mute and fullscreen. Another does not appear as a punishment.

**Dwell.** Onset 200 with no trail. From 200 ms to 650 ms the ring fills and a faint trail draws along the open cells from the token to that stone. The trail follows the corridor, does not cut a corner, and is not a hit area. Latch: the token walks that one open edge. The walk is animated. There is no jump. Then the lights update. Release or cancel before 650 ms: the token does not move, the trail clears, and the ring clears. There is no persistent latch.

**Wrong look.** Does nothing. No buzzer, no lost life, no return to the start. A dead-end stone is a correct use of the control, not a miss.

**Two-step.** Not used. Waypoint only. Do not dwell the start and the goal of a corridor as a pair. Do not dwell a path.

**Size.** Painted diameter is 160 px when every lit stone can keep a center-to-center gap of at least 320 px and an inset of at least 16 px. Otherwise the diameter is 112 px and the center gap is at least 224 px. Hit padding is at most 8 px and must not close the dead floor between stones. If 112 px stones would still crowd, regenerate. If that still fails, light one fewer stone. Never paint a stone under 112 px.

At 390 px the playfield is about 374 px wide, so two 160 px stones cannot keep a 320 px center gap. Use 112 px stones. Three 112 px stones fit if their centers are at least 224 px apart inside a floor of about 374 × 500. If the maze cannot do that, light two, or one, rather than shrink. Keep stones out of the outer 16 px. Younger bands get fewer stones and more floor, not a longer dwell.
